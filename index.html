Total time from truck arrival to WMS entry — work + all waiting buffers.

VA — Value-Added

Steps that physically transform or move goods (customer pays for this).

NVA — Non-Value-Added

Inspections, paperwork, and queueing — primary sources of process waste.

%PCE

Percentage of VA time in total Lead Time. Higher % = less process waste.
